Slide 1 · Context and Why Now

Premium leakage is a now problem — not a next-year project

AI Billing Intelligence for P&C insurers in India: from “manage bills” to predict, recommend, and recover.

Challenges and impact

  • Billing is reactive: invoices and generic reminders, no personalization.
  • Delinquency is found too late — after cash-flow stress, festive misses, or AutoPay gaps.
  • Impact: premium leakage, policy lapses, higher ops cost, weaker customer experience.

Strategic imperative

  • Every delayed action raises lapse risk on Motor, Health, and Home books.
  • India drivers: UPI-first payments, salary-credit timing, festive cash-flow cycles.
  • Leaders need explainable next-best-action — not another static overdue list.

Shift in operating model

  • From chasing bills to an AI command center that scores every policy.
  • AI proposes WhatsApp, call, installments, grace, or AutoPay — with predicted success.
  • Managers approve; agents get drafts. Work moves from guesswork to guided recovery.

Slide 2 · Current Challenges and Gaps

Today’s collection workflow cannot keep up

Existing process

  1. Generate invoice / due notice
  2. Send the same reminder to everyone
  3. Wait for payment or bounce
  4. Discover delinquency late
  5. Agent improvises recovery

Pain points & manual work

  • No risk ranking — high- and low-risk treated alike.
  • Agents draft emails and call scripts by hand.
  • Installment math and “what to offer” are tribal knowledge.
  • Managers lack a single approve-and-track decision trail.

Productivity, tech & business gaps

  • High-touch collection without prioritization.
  • No explainable risk score, next-best-action, or chat copilot.
  • Core systems store bills — they do not decide what happens next.
  • Business limit: leakage and lapses grow while CX and retention suffer.

Slide 3 · Vision and Future State

A billing command center that tells the team what to do next

What this project enables

  • Efficiency — rank at-risk policies; skip one-size-fits-all chasing.
  • Faster decisions — risk %, next action, predicted success in one screen.
  • Better CX — UPI AutoPay, salary-day timing, installments — not legal notices first.
  • Less effort — AI drafts email, call script, and 3/6/9 month plans.

Strategic outcomes

  • Recover predicted leakage before policies lapse.
  • Managers approve with an audit trail (Pending → Approved).
  • Agents act with explainable “why this score” — not a black box.
  • Simulator shows bulk recovery potential in one click.

Future-state operating model

  • People — manager approves; agent executes with scripts.
  • Process — dashboard → at-risk → analysis → approve → comms.
  • AI — rules score and plan; Gemini writes admin briefings and customer copy.
  • Work is guided recovery, not reactive bill chasing.

Slide 4 · Solution Overview

Working prototype — one Spring Boot jar

Architecture

Browser (Thymeleaf + Chart.js)
  → Spring Boot MVC + REST
    → AiFacade
         ⇄ Gemini (narratives, email, chat)
         ⇄ Rule engine (scores, plans)
      → Decision Service → H2
        → Dashboard / Analysis / Simulator

AI tools: Cursor (build lifecycle) · Gemini API (runtime) · rule engine (offline fallback)

Key capabilities

  1. Delinquency prediction — risk % from behaviour, income, claims, AutoPay, region.
  2. Collection recommendation — next-best action + predicted success.
  3. Explainable AI — admin briefing: why the score, mitigating signals.
  4. Email & call script — customer-facing drafts for India (UPI / Hinglish OK).
  5. Payment plans — 3 / 6 / 9 month options with a best-fit rationale.
  6. Chat copilot — floating Q&A on billing health and a named policy.
  7. Autonomous simulator — bulk scan, leakage, streamed recommendations.
