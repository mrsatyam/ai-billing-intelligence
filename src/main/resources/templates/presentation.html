The problem

Traditional billing is reactive

  • Invoices and generic reminders — no personalization
  • Delinquency discovered too late
  • Premium leakage & policy lapses
  • Agents lack explainable guidance

Result: revenue loss, higher ops cost, weaker customer experience.

Our solution

An insurance billing command center

Predict

Delinquency risk score per policy from behaviour, income, claims, AutoPay, region.

Recommend

WhatsApp, agent call, installments, grace, AutoPay discount — with predicted success.

Act

Draft email & call scripts, payment plans, manager Approve → workflow saved.
